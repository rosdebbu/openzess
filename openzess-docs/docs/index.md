## Why OpenZess?

OpenZess is not just another chatbot UI. It is an **autonomous operating system for AI agents** — a complete environment where self-improving agents execute code, browse the web, profile your habits, and collaborate across terminal and web interfaces.

Built by [Debjit Das (@rosdebbu)](https://github.com/rosdebbu), OpenZess gives developers total ownership over their AI workflow without vendor lock-in.
